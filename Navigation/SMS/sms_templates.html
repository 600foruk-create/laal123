Dynamic Placeholders

You can use the following tags in templates. They will be auto-replaced when sending:

  • {customer_name} - Client name
  • {opening_balance} - Starting balance
  • {receipts} - Received amount / total payments
  • {payments} - Payments amount
  • {net_balance} - Current outstanding balance
  • {company_name} - Your company name

Double click a tag above to copy or insert it into the message box.
